## 📋 POSITION OVERVIEW

We're more than aviation experts, we're pioneers. We challenge what's possible. From breaking the sound barrier to advanced tiltrotor systems. Today, Bell is shaping the future of aviation through specialized engineering.

**Your Mission:** Master Data and Procurement Systems

Bring your skills and experience as a Business Systems Analyst to join our team and drive automation and system integration within our procurement processes. In this role, you will collaborate with company leaders, developers, business users, software providers, and external integrators to identify, develop, and implement process automation opportunities across Bell's procurement systems.

**The Ideal Candidate:** Combines a strong understanding of procurement operations with technical expertise in Python scripting, API interaction, and data extraction to enhance efficiency, reduce manual effort, and ensure compliance. This position offers the opportunity to tackle complex process challenges, sharpen technical skills, and build leadership and communication experience while working closely with internal customers and cross-functional departments.

## 🎯 WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

### Core Responsibilities

1. **Design and implement automated solutions** to streamline supply chain processes and eliminate manual touchpoints.

2. **Integrate data and workflows** between Ariba, SAP, and legacy systems using APIs and Python scripts.

3. **Support the transition** of processes from CLM and SNC to Ariba.

4. **Partner with IT and functional teams** to gather requirements and deliver automation solutions that align with business needs.

5. **Develop, maintain, and document** Python scripts, SQL queries, and process automation logic.

6. **Perform data analysis and cleansing** to support operational reporting and decision-making.

7. **Troubleshoot and resolve** system or integration issues in collaboration with technical teams.

8. **Create and maintain** user documentation, process maps, and training materials.
