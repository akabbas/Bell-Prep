## 🎯 Success Strategy: The 3-Phase Question Framework

### PHASE 1: Days 1-30 → UNDERSTAND THE LANDSCAPE

**Your goal:** Get the full picture  
**Your mindset:** "I'm a learner, show me how it works"

#### Questions to Ask Your Lead/Supervisor

**SYSTEM UNDERSTANDING:**
1. "Can you walk me through a typical sourcing event from start to finish?"
2. "How do our systems talk to each other? (ECC → Ariba → SCC → Network)"
3. "What's the most common issue users face? Can you show me an example?"
4. "What would a 'good' vs 'bad' configuration look like in Ariba?"

**PROCESS UNDERSTANDING:**
5. "What's the process for a template change from test to production?"
6. "Who approves what? What are the approval layers?"
7. "What's the most critical thing I should NOT break?"
8. "What happens if something goes wrong? What's the rollback process?"

**PEOPLE UNDERSTANDING:**
9. "Who are the key stakeholders I should know?"
10. "Which users call most? What do they usually ask about?"
11. "Who's the expert on [system]? Can I talk to them?"
12. "What teams do we support? What do they do?"

**KNOWLEDGE GAPS:**
13. "What takes you the longest to explain to new hires?"
14. "What do I need to know that isn't in the documentation?"
15. "What's the ONE thing you wish I knew on day 1?"

---

### PHASE 2: Days 31-60 → BECOME COMPETENT

**Your goal:** Handle most tasks independently  
**Your mindset:** "I can figure this out, but I want to do it RIGHT"

#### Questions to Ask Your Lead/Supervisor

**SOLVING PROBLEMS YOURSELF:**
1. "I found [issue]. Here's what I think is happening. Am I on the right track?"
2. "I tried [solution]. Should I proceed, or is there a better way?"
3. "What would you do differently if you were handling this ticket?"
4. "Is there a pattern I should know about for [common issue]?"

**GOING DEEPER:**
5. "Can you show me the most complex scenario you've seen?"
6. "What configuration mistakes are hardest to undo?"
7. "How do you debug API issues? Can you walk me through your process?"
8. "What's the difference between a 'L1 ticket' and a 'L2 ticket'? Show me examples."

**MAKING IMPROVEMENTS:**
9. "I noticed [inefficiency]. What's the reason we do it this way?"
10. "Could [process] be automated? What would that take?"
11. "Are there documented workarounds for [known issue]?"
12. "What would make your job easier?"

**BUILDING YOUR SKILLS:**
13. "Can I take the next ticket you were going to handle? You supervise?"
14. "Can I shadow you on a production deployment?"
15. "What should I be reading to get better at [topic]?"

---

### PHASE 3: Days 61-90 → BECOME THE EXPERT

**Your goal:** Own processes, mentor others, drive improvement  
**Your mindset:** "I own this, how do I make it better?"

#### Questions to Ask Your Lead/Supervisor

**OWNERSHIP & IMPACT:**
1. "Which process or system area would be most valuable for me to own?"
2. "What process improvements would have the biggest impact?"
3. "What's broken or inefficient that nobody's gotten to?"
4. "If I could fix ONE thing, what should it be?"

**STRATEGIC THINKING:**
5. "Where are we going wrong? What are the biggest pain points?"
6. "What's your vision for improving the Ariba platform over the next year?"
7. "How does what we do here connect to Bell's larger strategy?"
8. "What's the ONE thing that would make the biggest difference?"

**LEADERSHIP:**
9. "Can I help train the new person next quarter?"
10. "Can I lead the project for [improvement]?"
11. "What do you see as my biggest strength? Where should I keep growing?"
12. "Am I ready for more complex tickets? What would prepare me?"

## 🔍 The "Smart Questions" Framework

### Smart Questions Have These Qualities:

1. **You've already tried to solve it**
   - ❌ "Why isn't this working?" (lazy)
   - ✅ "I tried X and Y, now I'm getting [error]. What should I try next?" (smart)

2. **You show your thinking**
   - ❌ "How do I configure templates?" (vague)
   - ✅ "I think templates need X and Y. Is that right?" (shows you're thinking)

3. **You respect their time**
   - ❌ "Can we talk about everything?" (vague)
   - ✅ "I have 3 specific questions, takes 10 min?" (respects boundaries)

4. **You follow up**
   - ❌ Ask, don't listen, never use the info
   - ✅ Ask, take notes, apply it, report back

5. **You solve similar problems yourself later**
   - ❌ "How do I fix [issue]?" (every time)
   - ✅ "You explained [issue] yesterday. I remembered and fixed it today." (smart)

## 🎯 The 3 Most Important Questions to Ask

If you only ask 3 things, ask these:

### Question 1: "What wins look like?"
**"What does success look like in your mind? What would make you think 'great hire'?"**

Why: You need to know the target. If you don't know what "good" looks like, you can't hit it.

### Question 2: "What's most valuable?"
**"What's the highest-value thing I could focus on right now?"**

Why: You're new. You can't do everything. Focus on what matters most to them.

### Question 3: "How do I know I'm ready?"
**"What milestones tell you I'm ready for [next level of responsibility]?"**

Why: You need clear criteria. This shows you're thinking about growth.

## 📝 What You Should ACTUALLY Be Doing (Besides Questions)

**Questions alone aren't enough. You need ACTION:**

### ✅ Every Single Day:

**1. DOCUMENT EVERYTHING**
- What you learned
- How to reproduce issues
- Solutions you found
- Patterns you noticed
- Best practices

Why: You build a personal knowledge base. You become the reference.

**2. REPEAT WHAT YOU LEARNED**
- "So if I understand correctly, [concept] works like [explanation]?"
- Why: Confirms understanding. Shows respect for their time.

**3. FOLLOW UP**
- "I used that tip you gave me and it worked! Here's what I found..."
- Why: Shows you actually listen. Builds trust.

**4. HELP SOMEONE ELSE**
- "I just learned this, can I help you with that?"
- Why: Sharing knowledge = leadership. Respect grows.

**5. NOTICE INEFFICIENCIES**
- "I noticed [process] takes 2 hours. I have an idea..."
- Why: Shows strategic thinking. Not just task-focused.

## 🚫 Questions You Should AVOID

**These hurt you:**

- ❌ "I don't know how to do anything" (vague, helpless)
- ❌ "Why are we using this old system?" (critical without understanding)
- ❌ "Can I just copy code from someone?" (lazy, no learning)
- ❌ "Is this something I should be doing?" (unclear on ownership)
- ❌ "Can you just do it for me?" (depends on them, doesn't grow you)

## 🎓 The Learning Cycle (What Actually Works)

**This is the pattern that creates real growth:**

1. **LISTEN** - Hear how they do it
2. **UNDERSTAND** - Ask clarifying questions
3. **DOCUMENT** - Write down what you learned
4. **REPEAT** - Explain it back to them
5. **PRACTICE** - Try it yourself (in test realm first!)
6. **REPORT** - Tell them how it went
7. **IMPROVE** - Ask where you can do better
8. **TEACH** - Help someone else learn it
9. **OPTIMIZE** - Find a better way
10. **LEAD** - Own this area now

**This isn't a one-time loop. This is your 90 days:**
- Days 1-30: Steps 1-4 (listen, understand, document, repeat)
- Days 31-60: Steps 5-7 (practice, report, improve)
- Days 61-90: Steps 8-10 (teach, optimize, lead)
